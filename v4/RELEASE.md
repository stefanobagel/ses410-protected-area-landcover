## Scientific and operational limits

No approved independent-reference park fit or final scientific qualification is
claimed. Baseline diagnostics use WorldCover2021 product-derived labels and
unweighted samples; no design-weighted uncertainty estimator is implemented.
Detailed legends,100m general exclusion,10km blocks/500m block separation,
coverage99% and100/50 sample/score targets remain provisional. Guard strips stay
in prediction, with a narrower assessment domain disclosed. The November2 study
established no optimum/smallest supported distance or causal inflation estimate.

Preview tiles are explicitly exploratory and display-scale dependent; canonical
sampling/export are10m. Point checks do not establish whole-map truth. Full-park
mapped-area calculations and30/60m derivatives have technical serialization
coverage but were not repeatedly exported for this bounded release. A timed
intended-user trial and review-labor study have not been performed.

Jiwei/team must resolve detailed legend definitions, appropriate evidence and
reference protocol/roles, independent development/final assets, assessment design
and weights, year-comparison scope and justified acceptance targets. These gaps
prevent scientific acceptance, while the software paths can be tested safely
with proxy and isolated synthetic data.
